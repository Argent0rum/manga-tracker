Sync from your reading sites

Reads how far you are on MangaDex, mgeko and Weeb Central and moves your progress here forward. It never moves anything back, and you see the list before anything changes.

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: ⟳ Sync to Next Chapter
  2. Open MangaDex, mgeko or Weeb Central while logged in (any page) and click the bookmark.
  3. When it finishes, press Send to Next Chapter, check the list, and press Apply. Do it once per site.
On a phone

Bookmark any page, edit that bookmark, and replace its address with the sync code (). Name it ncsync. Then on your reading site type ncsync in the address bar and tap the bookmark that shows up.

Where to open chapters first

"Read" opens the exact chapter when the release has a direct link (MangaDex or official). Otherwise it opens your page for that series on the first site below that has it.

Backup

Your progress is saved in this browser (and on AniList once connected). You can copy it to another device.
